Plan and timeline

The launch first. Then the classes, in two phases.

Six steps from your yes to class 55. Send us the production day that works for Danyel; once we confirm it, your deposit holds it. First cuts arrive within a week of each shoot, and a 24 to 48 hour rush is an optional addition.

  1. This week

    Confirm

    Production day confirmed, scope and counts set, NDA signed, deposit received. The deposit holds your production day.

  2. Before the shoot

    Prepare

    You send the mood board, brand files and scripts. We send back a look preview in your brand and the run of show, and book hair, makeup and styling.

  3. Date held on deposit

    Production day

    One day at the studio: the founder film, Danyel’s signature portrait and Two States diptych, 11 team portraits and the group photograph, and the 11 story videos. Behind the scenes is filmed all day.

  4. Within a week

    Launch edit

    Photo selects and first cuts, then final files in 16:9, 9:16 and 4:5, captioned.

  5. Classes in blocks

    The class phases

    Two classes per expert per day, on dates we set together. Raw footage and project files hand over at the end of each phase.

  6. From launch

    Each release

    One class and its two promos, ready ahead of its release date.

The films are the start. We build where they live too.

You told us Education and Community is your fourth division, and that each founder brings a network to women owners. We build the home for both, from the same studio that makes the films. One partner to brief, start to finish.
