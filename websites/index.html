What ties the three together

  • One look. Shared logo, fonts, colors and navigation, so moving between them feels seamless.
  • One record per woman. Every form, purchase and sign-up lands in your CRM, matched by email.
  • One set of numbers. Google Analytics and every ad pixel run through one tag manager.
  • One consent banner. Visitors choose once, and every site respects it.
  • You own everything. Every account, domain, file and login is in ASSET-U's name from day one.
  • Room to grow. Your own community platform can replace Kajabi later without touching the sites.

Why the store lives on Shopify: TikTok Shop, Instagram shopping, virtual try-on and AI shopping assistants are built as Shopify apps, and they only run on a Shopify storefront. Putting House of HER Hair there gives you all of them on day one, and it still sits inside the same brand system and analytics as everything else.

Found and measured from day one

Search, AI answers and ad tracking, set up across every site.

A beautiful site that no one can find, or whose ads can't be measured, leaves money on the table. This line sets up every profile and pixel, in ASSET-U's name, before the first ad runs.

  • Google Search ConsoleEvery site submitted and indexed, so you can see what women search to find you.
  • ChatGPT business profile NewASSET-U claimed and verified in ChatGPT's Business Hub, with the House of HER Hair catalog connected through Shopify.
  • Bing Webmaster ToolsAI search tools also draw on Bing's index, so this helps you show up in AI answers too.
  • Google ShoppingHouse of HER Hair products listed free in Google Shopping through Shopify.
  • Meta pixel and Conversions APIAd results for Facebook and Instagram, including sales that phones and ad blockers would otherwise hide. Domain verified.
  • TikTok pixel and Events APIThe same for TikTok ads and TikTok Shop. Domain verified.
  • LinkedIn Insight TagMeasures investor and partner traffic to the Enterprise site.
  • Google Analytics and Tag ManagerOne place for every tag, with the clicks from each site to Kajabi and Shopify followed through to the purchase.
  • Structured dataTells Google and AI tools who ASSET-U is, who Danyel is and what each product is. The groundwork for a Google knowledge panel for Danyel.
